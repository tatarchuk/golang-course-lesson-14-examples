Step 1.7, slide 43 last row and homework item 2: repeated connect/disconnect cycles by hand, before and
after the slide 42 review fixes. scripts/cycles.sh starts the given server binary on 127.0.0.1:18080 and runs
bin/wsclient twice: 3 cycles of 100 clients that leave with a close frame (1000), then 3 cycles of 100 that
drop the TCP connection without one. Every client sends one chat message and reads the others. The goroutine
counts come from the server (GET /debug/stats). Recorded on the Mac, 2026-10-30.

The fixed server queues up to 256 messages per client (gorilla's own chat example uses 256): here every
queue gets a burst of 100 at once, and the 64 of step 1.6 made the hub drop healthy readers as slow
consumers under this load. The drop itself is still pinned by TestSlowClientDroppedNotBlocking (queue of 2).
