2. SendBuffer. With the slow-client drop in place, a queue of 16 was too small for the test bursts: 20 clients
   that chat at once put 20 messages into every queue within microseconds, and the hub disconnected healthy
   readers with 1008 "slow consumer: send queue full" (TestConnectDisconnectCyclesNoLeak, cycle 3) - the policy
   working, with the wrong size. The default became 64, the value the slide 34 design proposes (step 1.7 raised it
   to 256 for slide 43's 100-client burst, see docs/slides/cycles.txt); the drop policy
   itself stays pinned by TestSlowClientDroppedNotBlocking with a queue of 2. TestPingsAndBroadcastsShareOneWriter
   sends a burst of 300 to one client, so it uses a queue of 512: it tests writers, not backpressure.
